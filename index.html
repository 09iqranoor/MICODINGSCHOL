ABOUT THE ACADEMY

Quality tech education, accessible to everyone

Mi Coding School is an online IT academy built for people who want to learn practical, job-ready technology skills without needing to relocate or attend a physical campus. We believe quality technology education should be accessible to everyone, regardless of location.

Our mission is to turn beginners into confident builders through live instruction, real projects, and ongoing mentorship. Our vision is to become the most trusted online technology academy for career-focused learners.
